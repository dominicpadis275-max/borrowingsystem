# borrowingsystem
# Project Brief: [borrowing system]
## 1. The problem
Who has the problem, and what is hard for them today? (2–3 sentences)
Students and school staff sometimes have difficulty borrowing school equipment because the borrowing process is done manually. It can be hard to track borrowed items, check equipment availability, and monitor return dates. Our system aims to make borrowing and returning school equipment easier, faster, and more organized.
## 2. Users and what they need to do

**Student**	View available equipment and submit borrowing requests.
**Teacher**	Borrow equipment for classroom activities and check borrowing status.
**Admin/Staff**	Manage equipment, approve borrowing requests, and monitor returned items.

## 3. The database behind it
Borrower	Stores information about students and teachers who borrow equipment.
Staff	Stores information about staff responsible for managing borrowing transactions.
Category	Stores equipment categories such as computers, projectors, and laboratory tools.
Equipment	Stores equipment details, quantity, and availability.
Borrowing	Stores borrowing transactions, including borrower, staff, and borrowing dates.
Return_Record	Stores information about returned equipment and return dates.
## 4. Must-have features (for the final project)
Add borrower information and register new users.
View the list of available school equipment.
Search equipment by name or category.
Submit borrowing requests for available equipment.
Approve or reject borrowing requests.
Record returned equipment and update its availability.
## 5. Nice-to-have features (only if there's time)
Generate borrowing and returning reports.
Display notifications for upcoming return dates.
View borrowing history of each student or teacher
## 6. Similar apps I looked at
